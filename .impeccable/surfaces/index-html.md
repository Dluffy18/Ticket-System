## Scope

The whole Violation Ticketing web app (`index.html`): Enforcer screens on phones outdoors; Office Admin and Verifier screens on desk PCs. Visitor mode: **Operate**.

## Audience and task

Enforcers record each pre-numbered paper Citation by its Control Number, in strict order, with photos and Violator Details, mid-street, one-handed, in sun or at night, often offline. Office Admins approve and switch Enforcer accounts. The Verifier marks Issued Citations Paid. Functional authority: `BUILD-PROMPT-FUNCTIONAL.md`.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The screen is the top copy of the paper form. The Control Number is the form's red numbering-machine serial, and the app is the ledger that accounts for every serial. It refuses the default government app: blue app bar, rounded white cards, pill badges.

OWN-WORLD: The printer's navy ink (#1C2B4A) for text, rules and the header band. White sheets on a pink-copy ground (#F4E1E5). Canary copy (#F7E9A8) for Not synced and warnings. Numbering red (#B3162B) only on Control Numbers and Void. Outcomes are rubber-stamp marks with a double ruled border in uppercase: green ISSUED/DONE, grey CANCELLED, red VOID, violet Treasury PAID. Archivo for everything, Courier Prime Bold for serials. Forms are boxed with 1px ink rules.

STORY: The Enforcer sees which number to write, matches it to the paper, records it, and trusts that nothing was lost. The admins see accounts and outcomes at a glance.
